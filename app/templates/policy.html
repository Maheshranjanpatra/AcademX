Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email. We additionally ask for your department and registration number. Your identity is tied to your university credentials for moderation purposes — even when your posts appear anonymous to other students.

We do not store your IP address, browsing history, or location data.

2. How Anonymity Works

When you post anonymously, other students see a randomly generated identifier (e.g., User_8472). Your real name is never visible to other users. Only platform administrators can link your anonymous posts to your real identity — and this is done strictly under the conditions listed below.

3. When Your Identity May Be Revealed

Your real identity will only be disclosed to third parties in the following specific situations:

  • Harassment, threats, or illegal content — If you use the platform to harass others, make threats, or post illegal content, admins may reveal your identity to address the issue.
  • Law enforcement (with a court order) — If law enforcement presents a legally binding warrant or court order, we will disclose the necessary information.

Note: Academic dishonesty (e.g., sharing exam papers) or spam alone do not automatically trigger identity disclosure.

4. How You'll Be Notified

If your identity is ever disclosed to a third party, we will notify you immediately (unless legally prohibited). The notification will include:

  • Who requested the disclosure (e.g., university authorities or law enforcement).
  • Why the disclosure was made.
  • A brief timeline of events leading to the disclosure.

5. Admin Accountability

Only authorized platform administrators can access your real identity. All such access is logged strictly for security purposes. We do not maintain permanent audit logs of identity disclosures to protect your privacy — only the minimum information (department, registration number, email) is stored as part of your user profile.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

If you delete your account, we will retain your data for one (1) month to allow for recovery in case of accidental deletion, or to assist with any ongoing investigations. After this period, all your personal data (including questions, answers, quiz attempts, and video progress) will be permanently erased from our systems.

7. Your Rights

  • Right to deletion — You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via the Contact page.
  • No automated data export — We do not offer a built-in data export tool. However, you may manually copy your questions and answers before deletion.

8. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your identity with other students.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as AcademX grows. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated terms. Significant changes will be communicated via email or a platform announcement.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or if you wish to request account deletion, reach out via our Contact page.
