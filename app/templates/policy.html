Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What We Collect

Guest sign-in does not ask for a name, email, password, Google account, or registration number. We store the department and avatar you choose, a randomly assigned pseudonymous ID, and content/activity associated with that ID so the application can provide its features.

Your browser sends network information to the hosting provider as part of normal web requests. Failed admin sign-in attempts are rate-limited using a keyed hash of the connecting address; only attempts from the most recent 15 minutes affect the limit, and old entries are cleaned up on admin sign-in requests.

2. How Anonymity Works

Public posts use your randomly assigned ID (for example, User_8472). This ID is a pseudonym, not a password or verified identity, and anyone using your current browser session can act as that guest profile. If you clear or let your browser session expire, you cannot recover the profile. We do not collect identity details that would let admins link the ID to your real-world identity.

3. Content Review

Questions and answers are available publicly when posted. Submitted video playlists, papers, and study materials are held for administrator review and appear in public listings only after approval. Administrators may inspect submissions and remove content that violates platform rules.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Contributions and activity remain associated with their pseudonymous profile while retained by the service. Admin deletion removes the profile and associated activity and submission records. Content that has been de-attributed may remain, and files already placed in external storage may require separate cleanup. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a post or uploaded file.

5. Your Rights

  • Right to deletion — You may request deletion of your pseudonymous profile and associated data via the Contact page.
  • No automated data export — We do not offer a built-in data export tool. However, you may manually copy your questions and answers before deletion.

6. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your identity with other students.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or marketing.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as AcademX grows. Please review it for changes.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or if you wish to request account deletion, reach out via our Contact page.
